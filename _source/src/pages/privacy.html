Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. Nordic Steam Co., 1420 Birchwood Ave, Suite 5, Portland OR 97209 is the controller of the personal data described here.

1. What we collect

You give us

  • Name, email address, phone number, billing and delivery addresses
  • Account credentials, if you create an account
  • Order contents, delivery notes and correspondence with our team
  • Review content, star ratings and any photos you upload with a review
  • Marketing preferences

Collected automatically

  • IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page
  • Pages viewed, products viewed, cart contents and session duration
  • Cookie identifiers (see section 6)

We do not store full card numbers. Card details are captured directly by our payment processor and never reach our servers.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with providers who need it to do their job for us:

  • Payment processors — to take payment and screen for fraud
  • Freight and parcel carriers — name, address and phone number, so they can book your delivery window
  • Email and helpdesk providers — to send order and support messages
  • Hosting and backup providers — who store the site and its database
  • Analytics providers — usage data, where you have accepted analytics cookies
  • Professional advisers and authorities — where the law requires it

4. Reviews are public

Anything you submit as a review — your display name, rating, review text and photos — is published on the site and is visible to anyone, including search engines. Your email address is never published. Use a first name and last initial if you would rather not be identifiable. You can ask us to remove your review at any time.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records — 7 years, as tax law requires
  • Account data — until you delete the account, then 30 days in backups
  • Support correspondence — 3 years
  • Published reviews — until you ask us to remove them
  • Marketing consent records — until withdrawn, plus 2 years as proof of consent
  • Server and analytics logs — 14 months

6. Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies keep you signed in and hold your cart — the site does not work without them. Analytics and marketing cookies are set only after you accept them in the banner, and you can change that choice at any time from the cookie link in the footer.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or port it to another provider. You can withdraw consent for marketing at any time. If you are in California, you also have the right to know what we collect and to opt out of "sharing" as CCPA defines it — we do not sell or share data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To exercise any of these, contact us through the contact page or call +1 (555) 014-7788. We respond within 30 days and will not charge you or treat you differently for asking.

8. Security

The site runs over TLS. Passwords are stored hashed and salted. Administrative access requires two-factor authentication, and we keep the platform and its plugins patched. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

9. Children

This store is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will post the new version here with a fresh date and, where the change affects how we use data you have already given us, email account holders directly.
