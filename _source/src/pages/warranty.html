Warranty policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. Cover runs from the delivery date and applies to the original purchaser.

1. Cover periods

Sauna cabins, barrels and structural timber5 years
Electric sauna heaters3 years residential / 1 year commercial
Wood-fired heaters and chimney components2 years
Steam generators2 years
Plunge tub shells5 years
Chillers, pumps and control boards2 years
Glass doors, hinges and hardware2 years
Heating elements and stones1 year
Accessories1 year

Commercial installations — gyms, spas, hotels, rentals — carry half the residential period on every category, reflecting duty cycle.

2. What's covered

Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use: element failure, control board failure, pump or compressor failure, delamination, structural timber splitting beyond normal seasoning, and hardware failure.

3. What's not covered

  • Normal ageing — colour change, minor surface checking in cedar, patina on metal
  • Damage from installation that does not meet the manual or local code
  • Electrical connection by anyone other than a licensed electrician
  • Water damage from a room that was not correctly sealed or drained
  • Scale damage in steam generators where no softener or descaling schedule was used
  • Freeze damage to plunge plumbing left full over winter
  • Misuse, accident, modification, or use outside stated parameters
  • Consumables — stones, gaskets, filters, fuses, lamps
  • Labour, crane hire, scaffolding, or the cost of removing and refitting an installed unit

4. Making a claim

  1. Contact us with your order number, the fault, and photos or a short video showing it.
  2. We diagnose remotely — most control-board and element faults are identifiable from a photo of the wiring and a fault code.
  3. Where a part is faulty, we ship a replacement free of charge, usually within 2 business days.
  4. Where the unit cannot be repaired in place, we arrange collection and either repair or replace it.

Keep your electrician's certificate or inspection sign-off. Claims on electrical components are far quicker to settle with it.

5. Replacement parts

We stock parts for every model we sell for the full warranty period and, in practice, several years beyond. Out-of-warranty parts are available at cost plus shipping.

6. Transfers

Warranty cover stays with the original purchaser and does not transfer with the property or a private sale. If you buy a house with one of our units in the garden, we will still sell you parts and talk you through repairs — it just is not a warranty claim.

7. Your statutory rights

This warranty is in addition to, and does not limit, the rights you have under consumer law regarding goods that are faulty or not as described.
