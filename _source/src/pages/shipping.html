Shipping policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. Applies to all orders placed on nordicsteam.example.

1. Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. Alaska, Hawaii and Canadian addresses are quoted individually because freight to those destinations is priced by the carrier rather than by our rate table — add the items to your cart and we'll email a quote within one business day.

2. Processing times

  • Accessories and small parts — dispatched within 1 business day.
  • Heaters, doors and controls — dispatched within 1–2 business days.
  • Saunas, steam generators and plunge tubs — dispatched within 3–10 business days depending on model. The lead time shown on the product page is the one that applies to your order.

Orders placed after 2pm PT, at weekends or on public holidays begin processing the next business day.

3. Delivery methods

Parcel

Items under 150 lb ship by parcel carrier, typically 2–5 business days in transit. A tracking number is emailed when the label is created.

Curbside freight — free on orders over $2,500

Large items ship palletised on an LTL freight carrier. The carrier telephones you to arrange a 4-hour delivery window and unloads with a lift gate at the end of your driveway or the nearest point a 53 ft trailer can safely reach. Moving the pallet from the curb is the customer's responsibility. Somebody over 18 must be present to sign.

Threshold delivery — $189

The carrier moves the pallet into your garage, carport or first dry interior space. Not available at all ZIP codes; availability is confirmed at checkout.

White glove delivery and assembly — from $749

Placed in your chosen position, assembled, and all packaging removed. Electrical connection is not included and must be completed by a licensed electrician.

4. Transit times

Freight transit is typically 3–7 business days after dispatch for the continental US, 7–14 business days to Canada. These are carrier estimates, not guarantees. Weather, port congestion and rural routing can extend them, and no delivery date is contractual.

5. Preparing for a freight delivery

  • Confirm a 53 ft trailer can reach your address. If it cannot, tell us before dispatch so we can route to a smaller vehicle or a terminal.
  • Have two able people and a hand truck or appliance dolly ready if you have chosen curbside.
  • Clear the path and the pad. Carriers will not move a pallet across soft ground.

Redelivery caused by a missed appointment or unreachable address is charged at the carrier's rate, usually $95–$185.

6. Damage, shortage and loss

Inspect the pallet before you sign. If there is visible damage, write "DAMAGED" on the carrier's delivery receipt before signing, photograph the crate and its contents, and contact us within 48 hours. We file the freight claim on your behalf and ship replacement parts at no cost.

A clean signature on the delivery receipt makes concealed-damage claims substantially harder to win, so it matters. If a shipment does not arrive within 10 business days of its scheduled window, contact us and we will trace it.

7. Split shipments

Orders containing both stocked and made-to-order items ship in parts, at no extra charge. You receive tracking for each part as it leaves.

8. Address changes and cancellations

Address changes are free before dispatch. After dispatch, carriers charge a reconsignment fee (typically $75–$150) which is passed on at cost. Orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full; after dispatch, the returns policy applies.

9. Duties and taxes

Canadian orders ship DDU — import duties, GST/HST and brokerage are payable by the recipient to the carrier on delivery.
