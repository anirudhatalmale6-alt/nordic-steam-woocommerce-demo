Accessibility statement

Last updated 1 November 2026.

1. Our commitment

We want anyone to be able to browse, compare and buy from this store, whatever device or assistive technology they use. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA across the site.

2. What we've done

  • Semantic headings and landmarks so screen readers can navigate by structure
  • Text alternatives on product images and meaningful icons
  • Colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and controls
  • Full keyboard operation — including the menu, filters, gallery, review form and star rating
  • Visible focus indicators on every interactive element
  • Form fields with real labels, and errors described in text rather than by colour alone
  • Layout that reflows to 320px and tolerates 200% zoom without loss of content
  • No content that flashes more than three times per second

3. Known limitations

  • Some manufacturer-supplied PDF manuals are not tagged for screen readers. Ask us and we will send the content in an accessible format.
  • The live chat widget is provided by a third party; its keyboard behaviour is outside our control. Phone and the contact form are always available as alternatives.
  • A small number of older customer review photos lack descriptive alt text.

4. Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site blocks you, tell us and we will fix it — and help you complete whatever you were trying to do in the meantime. Call +1 (555) 014-7788 or use the contact page. We respond within 2 business days.

5. How we test

Automated checks run on every release, and we test manually with keyboard-only navigation and a screen reader on the pages that matter most — home, category, product, cart and checkout. Full review annually.
