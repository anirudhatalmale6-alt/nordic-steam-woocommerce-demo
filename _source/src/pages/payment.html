Payment policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

1. Accepted methods

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Klarna — 4 interest-free instalments, subject to eligibility
  • Bank transfer (ACH) for orders over $2,500
  • Purchase orders from approved trade and government accounts

2. Currency

All prices and charges are in US dollars. If your card is issued in another currency, your bank sets the conversion rate and may add a foreign transaction fee that we neither control nor receive.

3. When you are charged

Card and wallet payments are captured in full at checkout. Bank transfers must clear before dispatch — allow 2 business days. Purchase orders are invoiced on net-30 terms once the account is approved.

4. Card security

Card details are entered directly into our payment processor's hosted fields and are tokenised before they reach us. We never see, store or transmit full card numbers, and there are none in our database. The site runs over TLS throughout and the checkout is PCI-DSS compliant through our processor.

5. Authorisation and fraud screening

Every order is screened automatically for fraud indicators. If a payment fails, the usual causes are an address mismatch, an incorrect CVV, or a card limit. We may ask for proof of address or cardholder identity on high-value or mismatched-address orders before dispatch, and we will cancel and refund in full rather than take a risk we are not comfortable with.

6. Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout from your delivery address. Tax-exempt and reseller customers should create an account and send us the exemption certificate before ordering so we can flag the account — we cannot refund tax retrospectively on an order that has already settled.

7. Failed and duplicate payments

A declined payment sometimes leaves a pending authorisation on your account. It is not a charge and drops off within 3–7 business days depending on your bank. If you see a genuine duplicate charge, contact us with both transaction dates and we will refund it the same business day.

8. Chargebacks

If something is wrong with your order, contact us first — we can almost always fix it faster than a chargeback, which takes 45–90 days to resolve. We contest chargebacks raised without prior contact, and we supply the carrier's proof of delivery and signature when we do.

9. Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Timing and conditions are set out in the returns policy.
