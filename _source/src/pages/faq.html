Frequently asked questions

Sizing, power, freight and warranty — the questions we get on the phone every week.

Choosing a sauna

How do I work out what size I need?
Allow roughly 2 ft of bench length per person, plus clearance for the heater and the door swing. Ceiling height matters more than floor area — over 7 ft and the heat pools above your head. Send us your room dimensions and we'll size it for you.
Traditional, infrared or steam — what's the difference?
Traditional heats the air with rocks at 160–195 °F and lets you pour water for humidity. Infrared warms your body directly at 120–150 °F, uses far less power and plugs into a standard outlet. Steam runs at 110–115 °F with 100% humidity and needs a sealed, tiled room. Traditional is the classic experience; infrared is the easiest retrofit.
Can a sauna go outdoors year-round?
Yes. Our outdoor units use kiln-dried cedar or thermally modified spruce, both of which handle freeze-thaw without splitting. In snow country add the metal roof option to shed load.

Power & installation

What electrical supply do I need?
Under 4.5kW usually runs on a 240V 30A circuit. 6–9kW needs 240V 40A. Over 12kW generally needs three-phase. Every heater must be hard-wired on its own GFCI-protected circuit by a licensed electrician — we send the wiring schedule with your order confirmation.
Do I need a permit?
The sauna itself rarely needs one; the electrical work usually does. Rules vary by county, so check with your local building department before your electrician starts.
Can I install it myself?
Yes for the cabin — pre-cut kits go together with two people in three to six hours depending on model. No for the electrical connection, which must be done by a licensed electrician to keep the warranty valid.
What foundation does an outdoor sauna need?
A level gravel pad, concrete patio, or a deck rated for 60 lb/ft². It must be level within ¼" across the footprint or the door won't seal properly.

Orders, freight & returns

How long does delivery take?
Accessories and heaters ship in 1–2 business days. Cabins and plunge tubs ship freight in 3–10 business days depending on model, then 3–7 days in transit. You'll get a tracking number and the carrier books a 4-hour window with you.
What's included in "free curbside delivery"?
The carrier unloads the pallet with a lift gate at the end of your driveway or the nearest accessible curb. Moving it from there is on you — or add threshold or white-glove delivery at checkout.
Can I return a sauna?
Yes, within 30 days of delivery if it's unassembled and in original packaging. Return freight and a 15% restocking fee apply on large items. Full detail on the returns page.
What if it arrives damaged?
Note the damage on the carrier's delivery paperwork before signing, photograph it, and contact us within 48 hours. We file the freight claim and send replacement parts — you don't deal with the carrier.

Reviews

Who can leave a review?
Anyone can post, but only customers with a matching completed order get the "Verified buyer" badge. We never delete a review for being negative — only for spam, abuse or anything off-topic.
Can I add photos to my review?
Yes — up to five images per review. Photo reviews are the ones other buyers find most useful, especially for installs in unusual spaces.
