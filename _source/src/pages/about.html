About Nordic Steam Co.

Heat and cold equipment, sized properly and supported after the sale.

We started in 2014 out of a workshop in Portland, building cedar benches for other people's saunas. Customers kept asking which heater to put in, and we kept answering — eventually it made more sense to stock the whole thing.

What we stock, and why

We carry around 195 products across nine categories. That's deliberately narrow. Every heater we list has been run in our own showroom units, and we drop anything with a warranty claim rate above 2%. If a manufacturer changes a controller mid-model-year without telling us, we stop selling it until we've tested the new one.

Sizing comes first

Most sauna disappointment comes from an undersized heater in an oversized room, or a 12kW unit on a panel that can't feed it. Before you buy anything over $2,000 we'd rather have a five-minute conversation about room volume, ceiling height, glass area and your breaker capacity. It costs us the odd upsell and saves everybody a return.

After the order

  • A wiring schedule and clearance drawing emailed with your confirmation, so your electrician can pre-run conduit
  • Freight booked within 24 hours, with the carrier calling you to agree a window
  • Replacement parts stocked for every model we sell, for the life of the warranty
  • A phone number that reaches somebody who has actually assembled the unit you bought

On reviews

We publish everything that isn't spam or abuse, including the three-star ones about freight carriers. We reply to every critical review in public. If a pattern shows up in reviews — a fiddly bracket, a confusing drawing — we fix the underlying thing rather than the review.
