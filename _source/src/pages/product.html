Built to sit outside

The barrel shape isn't styling. A round profile puts the heater's convection loop right along the ceiling and back down the walls, so the air keeps moving instead of stratifying into a hot ceiling and cold ankles. It also sheds rain and snow without a separate roof structure.

What's in the crate

  • Pre-cut cedar staves, cradles and end walls, numbered and drilled
  • Two 6ft benches, backrests and a duckboard floor
  • Kelvin 8kW heater, heater guard and 90 lb of vulcanite stones
  • Tempered bronze glass door, hinges and magnetic catch
  • Digital wall control, thermometer and stainless tension bands

Electrical

The 8kW heater needs a dedicated 240V 40A circuit on a GFCI breaker, hard-wired by a licensed electrician. We send the wiring schedule with your order confirmation so your electrician can pre-run the conduit before delivery day.

Can this sit directly on a wooden deck?
Yes, if the deck is rated for 60 lb/ft² and is level within ¼" across the footprint. The cradles spread the load across four bearing points.
Does the 8kW heater need three-phase power?
No — 240V single phase, 40A dedicated GFCI circuit. Three-phase is only needed from 12kW upward.
Can I add a wood-fired heater instead?
Yes. Swap to the wood-fired option at checkout and we ship the chimney kit and heat shield with it. Add roughly two hours to assembly.
How long does the cedar smell last?
Strongest for the first 20–30 sessions, then it settles. Splashing water on the stones brings it back.
