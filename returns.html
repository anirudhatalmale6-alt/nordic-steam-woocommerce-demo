Returns & refund policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. This policy sits alongside your statutory rights, which it does not limit.

1. The short version

30 days from delivery. Unused, unassembled, in original packaging. Small items come back at your cost; large freight items carry return shipping plus a 15% restocking fee. Anything that arrived damaged or faulty is on us, in full.

2. Return window

Requests must be raised within 30 calendar days of delivery. Start one from your account page or by contacting us with your order number. We issue an RMA number and return instructions — parcels sent back without an RMA cannot be matched to an order and may be refused.

3. Condition

Returned goods must be unused, unassembled and in the original packaging with all fittings, manuals and accessories. A sauna that has been assembled, wired in, or has had water poured on the stones cannot be returned as unused. Heaters that have been energised are treated as used.

4. Costs

Parcel items under 150 lbCustomer pays return shipping. No restocking fee.
Freight items (saunas, plunge tubs, generators)Customer pays return freight, plus a 15% restocking fee.
Damaged, faulty or wrong item sentWe pay everything, including collection. No restocking fee.
Custom, made-to-order or cut-to-size goodsNon-returnable unless faulty (see section 7).

The original outbound shipping charge is refunded only where the return is our fault. Where delivery was free because the order exceeded $2,500, the actual outbound freight cost is deducted from the refund on a voluntary return.

5. Damaged on arrival

Note the damage on the carrier's paperwork before signing, photograph it, and tell us within 48 hours. We ship replacement parts or arrange a full collection and replacement at our cost. See the shipping policy for what the carrier requires.

6. Faulty goods

If a product develops a fault, contact us with the order number, a description and — where possible — a photo or short video. Faults inside the warranty period are repaired or replaced free of charge. Faults reported within 30 days of delivery can be refunded instead, at your choice.

7. Non-returnable items

  • Custom-built cabins, cut-to-size cladding and bespoke bench work
  • Products assembled, installed, modified or wired in
  • Gift cards
  • Clearance items marked "final sale" on the product page

None of this affects your rights if the item is faulty or not as described.

8. Cancellations

Cancel free of charge any time before dispatch. Once a freight shipment has left our warehouse it is treated as a return under section 4. Made-to-order items may be cancelled within 24 hours of ordering; after that, production has usually started.

9. Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the returned goods arriving and passing inspection. Depending on your bank, funds appear in 3–10 further business days. We email you when the refund is issued.

Where a return is partial — for example one item from a multi-item order — any volume discount or free-shipping threshold that no longer applies is recalculated and adjusted against the refund.

10. Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order — that way you are not waiting for one to clear before the other ships.

11. How to start a return

  1. Contact us with your order number and what you'd like to return.
  2. We email an RMA number and the return address or collection booking.
  3. Repack the item in its original packaging with the RMA number visible on the outside.
  4. Ship it, or wait for the collection if we arranged one.
  5. We inspect on arrival and refund within 5 business days.
